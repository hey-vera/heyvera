# backlog/ -- Future Ideas & Incubator

Docs here are intentionally pre-decision.

They capture good ideas that are not yet active workstreams or canonical repo proposals. This is the right place for rough ideas to live before they are promoted, shaped, or retired.

## Use This Folder For

- brainstorms
- rough concepts
- future bets
- "not now" ideas
- internal idea salvage from older work

## Promotion Rule

When an idea becomes real:

1. move it to `internal/active/`
2. tighten the problem statement and boundary assumptions
3. write a repo-level proposal or ADR if needed
