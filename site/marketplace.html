How the Skill Economy Works

ClawNet's marketplace is a closed-loop credit economy. Credits flow in from real money (Stripe/USDC), circulate through the skill ecosystem, and accumulate as reputation and utility.

💳
Buy Credits
Users buy credits via Stripe (card) or USDC on Solana. 1 credit = $0.001. No monthly subscriptions — top up as needed.
🛒
Purchase Skills
Browse the marketplace, purchase a skill for its credit cost. Payment settles atomically in SQLite — no partial states, no race conditions.
⚡
Instant Execution
After purchase, invoke the skill immediately. Your variables fill the prompt template, the ClawNet orchestrator runs it, and you get a structured answer.
💰
Creator Earnings
97% of every purchase goes instantly to the skill author's credit balance. 3% is the platform fee. No delays, no invoicing.
🔒
Staking
Lock credits against your skill to boost its position in search results. Staked credits are returned after the lock period (1–365 days).
📊
Reputation
Each invocation records a success or failure. High success rate + staking score = better discovery ranking and more organic sales.
