## Key Positioning (Post-Split)

AID is a FAMILY of protocols, not one monolith:
- AID-Trust = the "one npm install" trust layer (competes with Mnemom, Trusta)
- AID-Receipt = the receipt standard (no direct competitor — receipt format is the real lock-in)
- AID-Settle = trust-gated commerce (competes with PayCrow, KAMIYO on settlement)

The receipt format (Protocol 2) creates network effects that scoring (Protocol 1) doesn't. Scoring is replicable. Receipt format is a standard that creates ecosystem lock-in.
