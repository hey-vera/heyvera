# ClawNet Product — Open Concerns

> Concerns specific to ClawNet product features (not protocol).

## ORANGE

### O5: Guardian Accountability (Who Watches the Watchers?)
Guardians scored by the same trust system they protect. Compromised oracle = compromised guardian scores. Precision tracking (Opus #9) is one mechanism, not a solution. Requires either economic staking (token catch-22) or external anchoring.

### O8: Autonomous Defense Needs Critical Mass
Defense layers need minimum agent populations to function:
- Immune response Level 1 (MONITOR): ~50+ active agents
- Immune response Level 3 (QUARANTINE): ~200+ active agents
- Guardian anti-concentration: ~500+ agents
- EigenTrust Sybil suppression: ~500+ agents with diverse interactions

### O11: Bus Factor = 1 [DIF-BLOCKING]
One person. DIF engagement requires sustained multi-week parallel workstreams. Actively blocking progress, not theoretical.

## YELLOW

### Feature Scope Creep
The product feature list is enormous. Risk of building features that don't contribute to Protocol 1 adoption. Every hour on guardians or seasons is an hour not spent on DIF submission or external outreach. Discipline: Protocol 1 first, product features after adoption.
