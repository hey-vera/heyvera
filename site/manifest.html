How It Works

Four checks. One call.

Manifest runs four independent analyses on every request and returns a unified verdict.

Verify

Is your data accurate?

Cross-references your data against independent sources. Catches stale prices, wrong numbers, and outdated metrics before you act on them.

Assess

Is your reasoning sound?

Checks if your premises are actually true, not just logical. Finds the gap between what you think is happening and what the data says.

Preflight

Will your action succeed?

Pre-flight checks for known risks, budget impact, and health status. Flags concentration risks, historical patterns, and execution hazards.

Remember

What happened last time?

Recalls past decisions on the same subject and tracks outcomes. Builds an unreplicable history that gets smarter with every check.
