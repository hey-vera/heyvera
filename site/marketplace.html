License & Terms

Your selected license determines how buyers can use the skill output:

MIT — Buyers can use, modify, and redistribute skill outputs freely. Attribution appreciated but not required.
MIT-0 — Same as MIT but explicitly waives attribution. Maximum freedom for buyers.
Apache 2.0 — Like MIT but includes a patent grant. Good for enterprise/commercial use.
GPL 3.0 — Outputs must remain open. Buyers who build on your skill must also share their work openly.
Proprietary — Buyers may only use outputs as-is. No modification, redistribution, or derivative works.

All skills are prompt templates executed on the ClawNet orchestration layer. You retain authorship. The 3% platform fee applies to all purchases. Earnings can be withdrawn as USDC or reinvested.
